# Kārlis Hūgenbergers: *Leelajs Kristaps jeb Kristus-nessejs* (1850)

*Latviski:* Kārļa Hūgenbergera dzejojums „Lielais Kristaps jeb Kristus nesējs“, diplomātiska
transkripcija vecajā rakstībā. Pirmā korektūra ir pabeigta:

The legend of St Christopher told in verse, by Kārlis Hūgenbergers (Karl Friedrich Jacob
Hugenberger, 1784–1860), pastor at Ārlava. Modern title: *Lielais Kristaps jeb Kristus nesējs*,
"Great Christopher, or the Christ-bearer".

## The orthography

Sixty years older than the Skalbe volumes in this repository, and spelt differently:

- **Doubled consonants** after a short vowel: `kuꞧꞧa`, `wezzaki`, `ſemme`, `wiꞥꞥu` (later
  *kuꞧa*, *wezaki*, *ſeme*).
- **Apostrophe for a dropped vowel**, mostly where the verse needs a syllable less: `mann'`,
  `darr'`, `Wiꞩꞩ'augſtakam` beside `Wiꞩꞩu-augſtakam`.
- The same phrase can be spelt more than one way: `Wiꞩꞩu-augſtakam`, `Wiꞩꞩ'augſtakam` and
  `Wiꞩsaugſtakam` (with a round `s` at the joint) all occur. There was no binding orthography
  yet; every form stays as printed.

Its word forms are listed apart from the Skalbe volumes, in `lexicon/forms-1850.tsv`.

## Layout of the files

The general rules are in [`../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md`](../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md). In this
book:

- **Verse without indent**, one line of verse per line. The print indents the first line of each
  stanza; that is not reproduced yet.
- **Stanzas** are separated by a blank line.
- **Song headings** (`Pirma dſeeꞩma.`) on a line of their own, followed by a blank line.

## The scans

`pages.tsv` links the image of every printed page in the library's viewer: page *p* is image
*p* + 4, e.g. p. 3 = `IMG00007`.
