Kad tee ſtahw pee juhrmallas,
Klibbajs rahda diſchlaiwas
Tam us juhꞧu, teiz: „tur ꞩallâs
Man irr walſtis mallu-mallâs.“

Kad tee ꞩkattahs dibbenî,
Wiltneeks teiz jo lepnaki:
„Wiꞩs ꞩchâ dſiłłumâ negantâ,
Kalpo man, irr manna manta.“

Ꞩullaiꞥam nu dſirdeht preeks,
Ka tam tahds irr waldineeks,
Kas tik warrens, baggats rahdahs,
Kas par wiꞥꞥu muhſcham gahdahs.

Zaurahm naktim neꞩꞩe ꞩcho,
Kà tam gribbahs, klauꞩa to;
Apſtahjees pee krohga-weetahm
Klibbajs ſuhd ais durwim zeetahm.

Ahrâ ꞩullains gaida pliks
Kamehr kungam isnahkt tiks.
Jebꞩchu tumꞩch, kas warr to baidiht?
Jebꞩchu ꞩalſt — ne apnihk gaidiht.
