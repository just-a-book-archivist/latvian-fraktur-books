Un daſchkahrt ilgi gaidijis,
Lihdſ kungs no krohgeem isnahzis;
Tur dſirdejs eekꞩchâ błauꞩchanu
Kà łauni Deewu ſaimotu, —

Tas teiza: „Wai, kas tas warr buht?
Ꞩcheem blehſcheem wajag ellê kłuht,
Kà plohꞩahs tur, kà nolahdahs!
Woi kungs ar tahdeem beedrojahs?“

Bet tuhdał atkal atrahwees,
Wiꞥꞩch teize: „mułꞣit peewiłłees!
Kungs gudrineeks un meiſteris:
Tew jaklauꞩa — tu ꞩullainis.“

Un daſchreiſ, kad no pilꞩata
To łaunajs ahr' isjahdija, —
Tur waimanas tam auꞩîs ꞩkann
Un atꞩkattitees tiktu gan.

Jo dſird, ka ꞩprehga, ꞩpahri luhſt,
Ka dumpis leels un ehkas gruhſt.
Gaiꞩch arr' pee debbeꞩs atꞩittahs,
Lai glahbj, lai glahbj! ꞩauz breeꞩmibâs.
