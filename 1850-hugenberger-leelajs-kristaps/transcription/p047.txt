Gaudeeꞥeem un ꞩlimneeziꞥeem
Wiꞥꞩch eet labpraht paligâ,
Teiz: „Kà darru nabbadſiꞥeem,
Mannam Kungam darru tà!“

Diwideꞩmitpirma dſeeꞩma.

Weendeen us wehlu wakkaru, —
Jo karſtâs deenâs ne-apſtahjis
Ar reiſneekeem zaur uppi gahjis —
Tam gribbahs noꞩnauſt brihtiꞥu.

Wiꞩs kluꞩs irr mehneꞩs-ꞩkaidrumâ,
Ne weenu dꞩihwu wairak manna, —
Arr' uppes-wilꞥu-kuſteꞩchana
To ꞩnaudeju nemohdina.

Tè kluꞩꞩumâ kas eeꞩauzahs,
Jo mihłi ꞩauz, ne lagsdigalla.
Tas ꞩauzejs teeꞩcham ohtrâ mallâ,
Kà behrniꞥꞩch tas tam isleekahs.
