Bet kalps tam buht — tam paklauꞩiht,
To ꞩchodeen ꞩpehꞩi isdarriht.
Pateeſi tewim rahdiꞩchu,
Kà kalpo tam ar ꞩpehzibu.

Tur uppe, rau, ꞩkreen appakꞩchâ,
Ta ꞩchaujahs aꞩchi — platta ta,
Ar behdahm un daudſ puhliꞥu
Ta baida Kriſtus draudſibu.

Tur ohtrâ mallâ łautiꞥi
Stahw, mett ar rohku bailigi,
Ꞩcho baſnizu gribb apmekleht,
Un mannu Kungu noꞩlaweht.

Bet uppe ſtrauja ſtraumehm ꞩkreen,
Ar pluhdeem baida nakt un deen',
Ka ne-usdrihkſtahs zauri briſt,
Kaut ꞩlahpes teem pehz Jeſus Kriſt.

Lihdſ kahda laiwa rohnahs kur,
Kas behduliꞩchus usꞥemm tur,
Zełł pahri tohs no turrenes,
Tohs ispeſtiht no nelaimes.
