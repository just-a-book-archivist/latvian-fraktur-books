To teizis, aistekk tezziꞥus,
Un isteizejam ruhpes rohdahs,
Kà tam ꞩcho wahrdu ꞩapraſt buhs,
Kurp leelajs gan tik ahtri dohdahs?

Ꞩchis tomehr prohjam ꞩkrehjis drihſ,
Bes ka ko buhtu lizzis ohmâ,
Ꞩchꞣeet, augſtako gan atraddihs
Un ꞩawâ mulꞣâ prahtâ dohma:

„Lihdſ ꞩchim pee neeka meiſtereem
Es ꞩawas deenas paſaudejis;
Naw gohds man bijis kalpoht teem,
Tik leels un ſtipris welti ꞩkrehjis.

Bet ꞩchim buhs mannam
Un meiſterim buht muhſchu mannu,
Kà labpraht tizzehs ꞩullaiꞥam,
Man usdohs augſtu darboꞩchanu!

Tad wiꞩs buhs labbi padarrihts
Un kam tik azzis, redſehs arri,
Ka wiꞩꞩur tizzams ꞩullainihts
Wiꞩsaugſtakam pa prahtam darri.“
