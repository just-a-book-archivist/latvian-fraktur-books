Us ꞩawu preedi atꞩpeedahs,
Kas appakꞩch ꞩwarra lohzijahs,
Kà meetus kahjas eemehje,
Lai tahs neꞩchłuktu bedritê.

Tà wihrs ar milſa-ſtiprumu,
Ka kauli drebb, ſteepj behrniꞥu,
Tam azzis peerê pahrgrohſahs
Un matti kahjâs pazełłahs.

Un lihdſ tas uppes-widdû nahk,
Ꞩirds-isbailês wiꞥꞩch luhgtees ꞩahk:
„Deews lai man lihdſ, us kammeꞩi
Man ꞩchꞣeet, es neꞩꞩu paꞩauli!“

Tè wiłꞥi pluhdôs aprimſtahs
Un wehji gaiꞩâ noſtahjahs,
Arr' ꞩwehtajs kluꞩꞩums apkahrt tà
Kà Deews gribb runnaht, ꞩluddina.

Klaht auꞩî wiꞥꞥam jauki ꞩkann
Tas debbeꞩs wahrds, un leelajs mann',
Ka daiła behrna balꞩite
To, ꞩamiꞩꞩuꞩchu, eepreeze.
