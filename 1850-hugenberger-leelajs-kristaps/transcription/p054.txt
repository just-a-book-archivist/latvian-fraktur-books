Arr' isteiz wiꞥꞩch pateeꞩigi,
Kà Deewa ꞩpehks eekꞩch Kriſtus bij,
Ka tas ꞩchaî paꞩaulitê nahzis
Un nabbadſiꞥeem kalpoht ꞩahzis.

Ka tas kà behrniꞥꞩch peedſimmis
Eekꞩch wahjibas irr dſihwojis;
Bet ꞩirdî glabbajs baggatibu,
Ko ꞩauzis tehwa mihleſtibu.

Tad brihnumu parahditajs
Irr bijis wiꞩꞩuwarrenajs.
Ka ſuhd wiꞩꞩ'ſtipru ꞩlaweꞩchana,
Kur ween ka wiꞥꞩch jo ꞩpehzigs manna.

Ka nahwe patte warrena,
Kas wiꞩꞩeem dſihweem draudeja,
No wiꞥꞥa warras patrihzeja,
Buhs laupijumu atdeweja.

Ka grehku parrads uswarrehts,
Zaur wiꞥꞥa mihlib' isdeldehts;
Ka to, kas preezajahs muhs ꞩpaidiht
Kà paꞩchu wellu wiꞥꞩch warr baidiht.
