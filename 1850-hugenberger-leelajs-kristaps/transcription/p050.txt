Arr' uppê wiłꞥi pazehlahs
Un lauſdamees tam usſchahwahs.
Naw pluhdi tahdi bijuꞩchi,
Lihdſ zełłeem jabreen uhdenî.

Un kà pee debbeꞩs dſihwiba,
Tà kuſtahs uppes-dſiłłumâ.
Tam ꞩchꞣeet, ka kas tam rahdiꞩees,
Kas naw wirs ſemmes gaddijees.

Prahts grohſahs tam ka ꞩapnotu,
Tik ko tas walda brihnumu;
Tam ꞩirdî auſt un dwehꞩelê
Kà jauꞩma, ko ne-ispratte.

Bet tahdai wiꞥꞩch arr' netizzahs,
No dohmahm allaſch atmohſtahs,
Lai dſiłłumôs warr buht tam nè
Kà gaddahs eekriſt nelaimê.

Jo weenadi ween ꞩmaggaki
Tas behrns tam noꞩpeeꞩch kammeꞩi.
Behrns, — tomehr tahdu naſtu, klau,
Ne muhſcham tas wehl neꞩꞩis naw.
