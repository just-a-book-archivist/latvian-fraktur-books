Jau ꞩenn par wiꞩꞩu paꞩauli
To eꞩmu meklejs ruhpigi, —
Mann' allaſch łaudis peeꞩmehje,
Tu teikꞩi, kur tas mahjo ꞩchè?“

Tam wezzajs: „Teiz, ko gribbi jell
No wiꞥꞥa? Ꞩakki man, kadehł
No tahlenes tu atnahzis,
Pehz mannu Kungu praꞩꞩijis?“

„Ja Kungs wiꞥꞩch tewim, mihłakajs, —
Ꞩchis wakkars man irr laimigajs.
Wedd mann' pee ta, lai eeraugu
Jell wiꞥꞥa ꞩwehtu waidſiꞥu!

Lai wiꞥꞥam eꞩmu kalpotajs:
Wiꞩꞩ labbakam — wiꞩꞩ labbakajs;
Wiꞩꞩ' augſtakam no ꞣehniꞥeem
Es tizzams kalps pahr tuhkſtoꞩcheem!“

„Mans dehls, tik weegli netohvam
Mehs klaht ꞩchim debbeꞩs-ꞣehniꞥam.
Wiꞥꞩch kuꞧꞧu gribb, ween aplaimo,
Tam ꞩird' un dwehꞩel' atjauno.
