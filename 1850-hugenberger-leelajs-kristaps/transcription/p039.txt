Lai ja-eet arri tahłumâ
Un ilgus gaddus tam pehz wiꞥꞥa,
Nei duꞩmas buhs, nei ꞩkummibiꞥa,
Nei gurris apſtahs zełłmallâ.

Ꞩcho mekleht wiꞥꞩch nemitteꞩees
Lai nahwes-nakts ir wiꞥꞥu baida,
Lai kaps un mirroꞥi to gaida,
Nei duꞩꞩehs, pirms tam isdewees.

Un ja ꞩchis ꞣehniꞥꞩch pillî miht,
Kur wahrtu-ꞩargs to nelaiſch eekꞩchâ,
Wiꞥꞩch ſtahwehs deen' un nakt' tur preekꞩchâ,
Un ne-apniks to ꞩagaidiht,

Nei pukkoꞩees, nei ſchehloꞩees,
Lihdſ ꞣehniꞥꞩch pats tam usꞩkattijis,
Par kalpu to ꞩew israudſijis.
Ꞩchim pawehl preekꞩch ta rabditees.

Tad ꞣehniꞥꞩch preezigs ꞩajuttihs,
Ka peenehmis buhs kalpu tahdu,
Kas łaunu wahrdiꞥu ne kabdu
Tam lihdſi eedams nepelnihs.
