    _Prinzeꞩe_ (ſiꞥkahrigi tuwojas eſaram).
_Ꞩprihditis_ (pahrgalwigi, klanàs prinzeꞩes preekꞩchâ). Dſihwo
weꞩela, prinzeꞩe Seltite! Wehlu tew — lai tewi ar
teeꞩcham Nelabais parauj! (Aiſpeld ar gulbiti).

    _Prinzeꞩe_. Ko? Kas? Wiꞥꞩch brauz projam!
(Dauſa pee pils wahrteem). Atdareet! Atdareet! Wahrtus
wałâ! Atdareet!

        Otrais ꞩkats.

Prinzeꞩe. Galma dahma. Ꞣehniꞥꞩch.

        1., 2., 3. galma kungi.

    _Ꞣehniꞥꞩch_ (ar galma kungeem iſ pils). Kas ir?
Kas te tà dauſas nakts laikâ?

    _Prinzeꞩe_ (rahda uſ eſaru). Wiꞥꞩch brauz projam —
tur — — tur! Un nowehleja, lai mani welns paraujot!

    _Wiꞩi_ (ꞩkatas ar iſſteepteem kakleem).

    _1. galma kgs_. Es nekà newaru ꞩaredſet!

    _Prinzeꞩe_. Newar ꞩaredſet! Kas ta' jaredſ!
Dſenatees pakał! Ꞩkreeneet, brauzeet, jahjeet!

    _Ꞣehniꞥꞩch_. Rahmi! rahmi! Aiſꞩkrehja pats
projam. Labi! Ko wiꞥꞩch mums tagad wairs war darit!

    _Prinzeꞩe_. Kà wiꞥꞩch newar darit! (Ar pahrleezibu).
Wiꞥꞩch wiꞩu ko war darit!

    _1. galma kgs_. Wiꞥꞩch war gan darit. Wiꞥam
