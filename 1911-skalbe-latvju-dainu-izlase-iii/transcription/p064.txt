Tiht man tihk tihrumâ,
Tihk meſchiꞥa maliꞥâ:
Tihrumâ rudſi, meeſchi,
Meſchâ waꞩka ritenits.

*

Trihs rihtiꞥi ꞩaule lehza
Zaur oſola lapiꞥam;
Trihs gadiꞥi tautu dehls
Mani jaunu bildinaja.

Trim ſtareem upe tek
Selta ſirni guhſtidama;
Trim zełeem tautas jahja
Man maſai meitiꞥai.

Tumꞩâ redſu dſiłuhdeni,
Dwehꞩeliꞩchu ꞥehmajiꞥu;
Tahłu redſu tautu dehlu,
Aꞩariꞥu dſehrajiꞥu.

Uſ manim oꞩchi lihka,
Uſ manim oſoliꞥi;
Uſ manim Laime greeſa
Diſchanos tehwa dehlus.

*

Uſ manim puiꞩchi krita,
Kà uſ ogu wahzelites;
Waj mahmiꞥa man maſai
Medû muti maſgajuꞩe.

*

Wała man ꞩchowaꞩar,
Wała zitu waꞩariꞥu;
Wehl ij manas wilnanites
Seed aitiꞥas mugurâ.
