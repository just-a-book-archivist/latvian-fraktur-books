No roſiꞩchu lapiꞥam;
Jauni puiꞩchi raudſijàs
Zaur roſiꞩchu lapiꞥam.

Wiꞩi ſeedi waꞩarâ,
Wiꞩus wiju waiꞥagâ;
Ahbel's ſeedu ween newiju,
Tee raud gauſchi walkajot.

*

Kas tur ꞩpihd, kas tur wiſ
Paegliꞩchu kalniꞥâ?
Brahłam ꞩpihd ſtikla logi,
Mahꞩai ꞩkahrda wainadſiꞥꞩch.

*

Kas ziteem behdas bija,
Kad es ilgi waiꞥagâ?
Ꞩawu laiku eewas ſeed,
Ꞩawu laiku ahbelites;
Ꞩawu laiku es walkaju
Ꞩawu roſchu wainadſiꞥu.

*

Kundſiꞥꞩch leels, kundſiꞥꞩch maſs,
Bahliꞥꞩch ꞥehma zepuriti;
Lai nahk wiꞩi Rigas kungi,
Es neꞥehmu wainadſiꞥa.
*

Kur, ꞩaulite, darbu ꞥehmi
Tahdu gaꞧu waꞩariꞥu?
— Pinu roſchu wainadſiꞥu
Apkahrt meeſchu lihdumiꞥu.
