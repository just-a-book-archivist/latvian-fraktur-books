Ꞩchuj, brahliti, man kurpites
Ar ꞩudraba ꞩprahdſitem,
Lai es waru tautu dehlu
Ar kahjiꞥu kaitinat.
*
Pupinai raibi ſeedi,
Man raibaka willainite.
Kas, pupiꞥa, tew raiboja?
Man raiboja mahmuliꞥa.
*

Ꞩedſchos baltu wilnatniti,
Ꞩehdos eewas kruhmiꞥâ;
Eewiꞥai balti ſeedi,

Man bij balta wilnatnite.

Kas, eewiꞥ, tew apꞩedſe?

Man apꞩedſa mahmuliꞥa.
**

Sihlitei, ſchubitei
Paberam linꞩehkliꞥu;
Jaunajàmi mahꞩiꞥam
Pa ꞩaujiꞥai ꞩidrabiꞥa.

Ꞩkanet ꞩkan man małot,
Ꞩkanet ꞩkan welejot:
Sihles ꞩkan man małot,
Gredſeniꞥi welejot.

*

Ꞩmilga auga atmatâ,
Es ꞩawos brahliꞩchos;
Ꞩmilga raꞩu nepaneꞩa,
Es brahliꞩchu ꞩudrabiꞥa.

Aiſ Daugawas melni meſchi
Dſeltaneem ſeediꞥeem.
