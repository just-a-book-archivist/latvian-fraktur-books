Ꞩpihdej' mans wainadſiꞥꞩch
Zaur dewiꞥu glahſchu logu.
Ꞩweꞩcha mahte, zeldamàs,
Ꞩak' ꞩaulite uſlehkuꞩe.

*

Gani, gani, tautu meita,
Pa manàm ganibam;
Gan tu pate noganiꞩi
Ꞩawu ſihłu wainadſiꞥu.

*

Gani gowis, bahleliꞥ,
Lai es wiju wainadſiꞥu:
Ꞩkaiſti ſeed pureniꞥi
Awotiꞥa maliꞥâ.

Ganos gahju, galwu tinu,
Leepâ kahru wainadſiꞥu.
Trihs gadiꞥus ta leepiꞥa
Selta ſeedus noſeedeja.

*

Greeſe greeſa rudſu lauku,
Tauteets manu wainadſiꞥu.
Greeſ, tauteeti, dadſchu lapu,
Negreeſ manu wainadſiꞥu.

*

Ik rihtiꞥa ſelta raꞩa
Bahleliꞥa pagalmâ;
Kur eedama, tezedama,
Noꞩkaloju wainadſiꞥu.

*

Jau manam waiꞥagam
Weena puꞩe nowihtuꞩe;
Lai nowihta otra puꞩe,
Kad naw laba ꞥehmajiꞥa.
