Dehł ꞩihkuma bahleliꞥi
Nelaiſch mani druwiꞥâ,
Lai bitite nenoneſtu
Draweneeka dehliꞥam.

Dod, Deewiꞥ, ko dodams,
Dod ar bahrdu arajiꞥu!
Es guleꞩchu ſem bahrdiꞥas
Kà ſem luhꞩcha kaſchoziꞥa.

Dod, mahmiꞥa, kam dodama,
Nedod mani arajam:
Arajam ꞩmagas kahjas,
Pilnas ꞩmilꞩchu peebiruꞩchas.

Diwreiſ mihti gredſentiꞥi.

*

*

Dod, mahmiꞥa, kam dodama,
Nedod mani kurpneekam:
Kurpneeziꞥa dwehꞩelite
Ihlentiꞥa galiꞥâ.
