Dod, mahmiꞥa, kam dodama,
Nedod mani oſolam;
Oſolami platas lapas,
Neredſ ꞩauli uſlezam.
Dod labali behrſiꞥam,
Tam lapiꞥas ꞩudrabotas,
Kad ꞩaulite ꞩpoſcha lehza,
Tad lapiꞥas atꞩpihdeja.

*

Ai, Laimiꞥa, neꞩchkir mani
No tihkama tehwa dehla;
Ꞩchkir meſchâ ꞩihkus putnus,
Juhꞧâ baltus gaigaliꞩchus.

Nihktin nihka tas kalniꞥꞩch,
Kur mehs diwi runajàm;
Puꞩcham luhſt dſelſa ꞩleegꞩnis,
Kur mehs diwi ꞩchkihramees.

*

Pahri eeꞩchu par upiti, —
Nepalikꞩchu ꞩchaî puꞩê, —
Pee maiſites arajiꞥa,

Pee galwiꞥas glaudejiꞥa.

*

Pahri, pahri par upiti,
Ꞩchai puꞩê nepalikꞩchu:
Ꞩchai puꞩê bahrgi kungi,
Netikuꞩchi tehwa dehli.

*

Patiht man meeſchu druwa
Dſeltenàm wahrpiꞥam;
Patihk man tas puiꞩitis
Dſelteneem matiꞥeem.
