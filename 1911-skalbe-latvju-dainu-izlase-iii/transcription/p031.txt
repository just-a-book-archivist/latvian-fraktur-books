Dſeltainà zeelawiꞥa
Augſtu neꞩa zekuliꞥu;
Tà es ar', tà es ar',
Bahliꞥos dſihwodama.

*

Es ꞩawos bahliꞥos
Ꞩkanedama ween ſtaigaju:
Ꞩkan ſihlites, ꞩkan krellites,
Ꞩkan wiſułu wainadſiꞥꞩch.

Gan paſinu to meitiꞥu,
Kuꞧa tehwu klauꞩijuꞩi:
Lihdſ ſemei bruhni ꞩwahrki,
Lihdſ joſtai ꞩudrabiꞥꞩch.

*

Gꞧuhtas manas willainites,
Oſoliꞥa gꞧuhtumâ:
Wiꞩi ſtuhri peeloziti
Tehwa wezu dahlderiꞩchu.

*

Kahda kuꞧa deena bija,
Tahdu ꞩedſu willainiti:
Ꞩaules deenu baltu ꞩedſu,
Leetus deenu peleko.

*

Manas baltas willainites
Dſihpariꞥu nepaneꞩa;
Wehl brahliꞩchi leelijàs,
Selteem ſtuhrus kaldinat.

*

Naw wiꞩàm irbitem
Paſeltiti ꞩpahrnu gali;

Naw wiꞩàm meitiꞥam
Mahtes auſtas willainites.
