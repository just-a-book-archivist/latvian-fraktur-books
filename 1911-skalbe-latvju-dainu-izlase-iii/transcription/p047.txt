Apałꞩch kalns, ſałi mauri
Tautu dehla ꞩehtiꞥâ;
Tur buhs labi, kad aiſeeꞩchu
Willainites wehꞩinat.

*

Apkahrt kalnu ꞩaule gahja,
Gaꞧas deenas gribedama;
Apkahrt kalnu tautas jahja,
Gaida mani uſaugam.

*

Ar ſihliti ſiꞥu laidu
Ꞩawam linu arajam,

Lai tas nahza ꞩchorudeni,
Lai ilgaki negaidija.
*

Audſ, audſ, eglite,
Zeliꞥa malâ;

Kad mani wedìs,
Tad tewi lauſìs.
*

Audſ, behrſiꞥi, ſems un kuplis,
Eeꞩchu tewî ꞩlotu greeſt;
Audſ, tauteeti, lehns un gudris,
Buhꞩchu tawa lihgawiꞥa.
*

Audſin augu es, mahmiꞥa,
Deewiꞥꞩch ſina, kam es augu,
Waj es augu tautiꞥam,
Waj ſałàm welenam.

Augſtu kahpu, ſemu kahpu
Puiꞩeniꞥu luhkotees;
Augſtajâ apꞩitê,

Tur waiꞥagu darinaju.
