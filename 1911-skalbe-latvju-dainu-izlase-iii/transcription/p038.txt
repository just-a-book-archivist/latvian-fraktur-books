Wiꞩa tautu atmatiꞥa
Dſeltenàm puꞣitem.

*

Laideet, brahłi, ꞩaw' mahꞩiꞥu
Ar ꞩauliti ꞩehtinâ:
Wakars nahza, miglu meta,
Raꞩa bira waiꞥagâ.
**

Manis ſihłu wainadſiꞥis
Dſiłas juhꞧas dibenâi;
Mans maiſites arajiꞥis
Tahlejâs tautiꞥâs.

*

Ne tee wiꞩi ꞩkaidri meeſchi,
Ko wehjê wehꞩinaja;
Ne tas wiꞩas ꞩkaidras meitas,
Kas walkaja wainadſiꞥu.

*

Peezu brahłu mahꞩa biju,
Peezu mahrku wainadſiꞥꞩch:
Wiꞩi peezi bahleliꞥi
Pa mahrkai ꞩametuꞩchi.

Pinu, pinu, nenopinu
Ahbel'ſeedu wainadſiꞥu,
Ahbełſeedu wainadſiꞥꞩch —
Gꞧuhts muhſchiꞥꞩch dſihwojot.

*

Puꞣu wiju waiꞥadſiꞥu,
Sihłu kahru klehtiꞥâ;
Lai ſtahw ſihłu klehtiꞥâ,
Kamehr puꞣes noſeedeja.
