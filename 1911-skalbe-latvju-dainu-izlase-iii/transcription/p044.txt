Ꞩilta ꞩaule, karſta ꞩaule,

Diw' ꞩudraba gredſeniꞥi.

Tàm dſihwot kaimiꞥos:
Bites druwu neſleedeja,

Ai, aꞩo ſobeniꞥ,

Ai, ꞩmuidrais augumiꞥ,
Kam ar godu nedſihwoji.

Glaba ꞩawu augumiꞥu!
— Ai, godigs tehwa dehls,
Palihdſ pats paglabat.

— Ai, godigs tehwa dehls,
