Maſgà rihta raꞩiꞥâ,

Schauj ꞩudraba behrſiꞥâ.
*

Dſihràs mani tà lozit,

Kà wehjiꞥis ꞩmilgu loka;

Es nebiju tà auguꞩe,

Kà ꞩmildſiꞥa dſedſeedâ.
*

Abi diwi mehs raudam
Ar to tautu neweikliti:

Wiꞥꞩch raud mani gribedams,

Es pee wiꞥa neeedama.

*

Ai, Deewiꞥ, es negribu
Melnu galwu arajiꞥu;
Dſeltaneem matiꞥeem,
Ꞩarkaneem waidſiꞥeem.

*

Wai, Deewiꞥ, ko dariꞩchu,
Grib atraitnis mani jemt?
Ꞩweeſchu ꞩawu gredſentiꞥu
Ꞩihkâi kahrklu kruhmiꞥâi,
Mauzu ꞩawu wainadſiꞥu
Sałas needres galiꞥâi,
Laiſchos pate upitêi
Rauduwiꞩchu pulziꞥâi,
Labak upes rauduwite,

Ne atraiꞩcha lihgawiꞥa.

Aiſupneeki bahleliꞥi,
Zełat mani par upiti:
Man puꞣites, man roſites
Aiſ upites jarawè.
