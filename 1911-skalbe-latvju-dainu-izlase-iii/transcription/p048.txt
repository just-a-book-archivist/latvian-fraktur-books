Baltahbula lihzi bridu
Pee Laimites luhgdamees,
Lai ta mani newehleja
Leelam krogus dſehrajam.

*

Baltu puꞣu guni kuhru
Eſeriꞥa maliꞥâ;
Ne ta dega, ne ta dſiꞩa,
Laiſtitees laiſtijàs.

*

Wihru, manu wihreniti,
Pati tewi audſinaju:

Pee baſnizas ꞩeeru dewu,
Ganidama gabaliꞥu.

Bitei ꞩpahrni nodiluꞩchi,
Puꞣitês loſchꞥajot;
Puiꞩcham mati noꞩirmojꞩchi,
Pehz manim tihkojot.

*

Bitit, tawu weeglumiꞥu,
Tew nelihka ahboliꞥꞩch;
Man eelihka kalna mala
Ar tauteeti runajot.

Briſt purwâ, waj nebriſt,

Baltas ſeꞣes kahjiꞥâ?

Eet tautâs, waj neeet,

Bahrga tautu mahmuliꞥa?
