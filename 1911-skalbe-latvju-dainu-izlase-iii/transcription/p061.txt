Wainadſiꞥa walkadama;
Tihra mana dwehꞩelite
Kà tehrauda gabaliꞥꞩch.

*

Pusrihtâ ꞩaule lehza
Pura behrſa galiꞥâ,
Puswaꞩaras tautu dehls
Raud uſ manu augumiꞥu.
Raud' uſ Laimi, tautu dehls,
Ne uſ manu augumiꞥu,
Buht' Laimiꞥa wehlejuꞩe,
Buht' es tawa lihgawiꞥa.

*

Rudas bites, mellas bites
Kriht pee mana wainadſiꞥa;
Ꞩihwas tautas, lehnas tautas
Jahja mani luhkotees.

*

Ꞩak' teeꞩcham, tautu dehls,
Kur tu mani noluhkoji?
Waj druwâ, waj pławâ,
Waj pee linu ehrkuliꞩcha?

— Ne druwâ, ne pławâ,
Ne pee linu ehrkuliꞩcha:
Weenu ꞩwehtu wakariꞥu
Par plahniꞥu pahrejot.

*

Ꞩargees, linu zeelawiꞥa,

No peleka wanadſiꞥa,
Kà es ſinu ꞩargatees
No dſehraja tehwa dehla.
