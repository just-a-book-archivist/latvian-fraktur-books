Nau ꞩeewìnas, nau behrnìnu
Aꞩarìnu raudataju;
Draugi, radi pawadija,
Tee aꞩaru neraudaja.
*

Wilkam labi, wilkam labi,
Ne tam wahꞡu, ne ragawu,
Panehmees ahſcha ragu,

Eet pa meſchu tauredams.
Puiꞩcham labi, puiꞩcham labi,
Ne tam ꞩeewas, ne behrniꞥu,
Panehmees ſtabuliti,
Gana ſirgus ꞩwilpodams.

*

Ai, Laimiꞥa lizejiꞥa,

Kam tu mani maſu liki!
Wiꞩi mani matu gali
Raꞩiꞥâ nomirkuꞩchi.

*

Apałꞩch puika es iſaugu,
Apałꞩch mans kumeliꞥꞩch,
Apał' mana tehwa ſeme
Kà weens waꞩka ritentiꞥꞩch.

*

Dehli auga kà oſoli
Augſta kalna galiꞥâ;
Meitenites lejiꞥâ
Kà needrites ꞩchuhpojàs.

*

Es iſaugu leela meita,
Lihdſ meſchiꞥa galiꞥeem;
Kad uſliku waiꞥaziꞥu, —
Pahri meſcha galiꞥeem.
