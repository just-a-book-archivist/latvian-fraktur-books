Kas man dewa to ahbolu,
Kas ahbeles galiꞥâ?
Kas man dewa to meitiꞥu,
Kas man tika prahtiꞥam?
*

Lihdſ ſemei laſdiꞥꞩch ſeed
Dſeltaneem ſeediꞥeem;
Lihdſ ꞩirdij man mihleja
Tautu meita ſeltainite.

*

Lihku lotꞩchu upe tek,
Uſ kraſteem eewas ſeed,
Lihku lotꞩchu tas zeliꞥꞩch,
Kur aug mana lihgawiꞥa.

*

Ne tas manis arajiꞥis,
Kas ap mani glaudijàs;
Tas buhs manis arajiꞥis,
Kas no manis wairijàs.

*

Neweenam neꞩaziju,
Kur aug mana lihgawiꞥa:
Sem eglites, ſem leepiꞥas
Sakâ roſchu dahrſiꞥâ.

*

Pahr upiti melli alkꞩchꞥi
Ꞩudrabiꞥa lapiꞥam;

Gubr upiti man uſauga
Ꞩudrabiꞥa walkataja.
*

Patihk man ta meitiꞥa,
Patihk wiꞥas walodiꞥa:
Kà needrite ta meitiꞥa,
Kà ꞩkujiꞥa walodiꞥa.
