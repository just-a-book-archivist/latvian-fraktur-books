III, *Dſeeꞩmas un jauniba* (modern: *Dziesmas un jaunība*, "Songs and youth").

## Layout of the files

The general rules are in [`../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md`](../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md). In this
book:

- **Verse without indent**, one line of verse per line. Indented only where the print indents.
- **Stanzas** are separated by a blank line where the print separates them by space.
- **Songs** are separated by the asterisk the print sets between them, on a line of its own with a
  blank line before and after, so each daina stays a unit.
- **Half-titles** (pp. 3 and 23) as printed, line by line.

## The scans

`pages.tsv` gives the image for every printed page: page *p* is image *p* + 4 of the Internet
Archive's JPEG 2000 set (`latvjudainuizlas00skaluoft_jp2.zip`), e.g. p. 9 =
`latvjudainuizlas00skaluoft_0013.jp2`.
