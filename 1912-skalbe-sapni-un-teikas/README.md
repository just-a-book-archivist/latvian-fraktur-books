## State

**Second proofreading complete** (24 October 2026): all 1,229 printed lines compared with their
image on the scan, for punctuation, line breaks, stanza breaks and lost or doubled lines. On the
way about 45 words were corrected (mostly strokes and accents the first pass had missed;

as `..` or `.`. Before that, 27 words were checked against the same words in the other Skalbe
volumes and corrected at the image (e.g. `ꞩapꞥo` for `ꞩapno`).

**First proofreading complete** (10 October 2026): every word compared with its image on the
scan and confirmed or corrected; every stroke and accent decided at the image.

## PDFs and plain text

Typeset versions are attached to the release [`sapni-2-lesung`](https://github.com/just-a-book-archivist/latvian-fraktur-books/releases/tag/sapni-2-lesung) and on the Internet Archive (24 October 2026): <https://archive.org/details/skalbe-1912-sapni-un-teikas-edition>. A reading version (the poems
set anew, every line of verse on a line of its own, a table of contents at the end) and a
diplomatic version (page by page, line by line), each in roman type and in Fraktur, as PDF/A,
and both as plain text. In the Fraktur PDFs the stroked letters are drawn over their base
letter, so their text layer holds the base letter (`ſ` for `ꞩ`).

The repository as it stood at this release is archived on Zenodo, citable as
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.23623980](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23623980).

## Layout of the files

The general rules are in [`../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md`](../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md). In this
book:

- **One line of verse per line.** The first line of each stanza is indented by four spaces, as
  in the print; the other lines have no indent. A stanza that runs on from the previous page
  starts without indent.
- **Stanzas** are separated by a blank line.
- **Poem titles** on a line of their own with a blank line before and after. Titles of several
  lines, numbers over parts of a poem (`I`, `II` …) and dedications in brackets stay together as
  one block. Bold type is not marked.
- **Untitled poems** begin with three asterisks, written `* * *`, followed by a blank line.
- **Place and year** under a poem (p. 10) on a line of their own, after a blank line.

## The scans

`pages.tsv` gives the image for every printed page: page *p* is image *p* + 6 of the Internet
Archive's JPEG 2000 set (`sapiunteikasdzej00skaluoft_jp2.zip`), e.g. p. 7 =
`sapiunteikasdzej00skaluoft_0013.jp2`.
