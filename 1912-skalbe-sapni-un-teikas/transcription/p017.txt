Meſcha  krodſiꞥꞩch

Sem eglem wehꞩâ ehnâ
Guł meſcha krodſiꞥꞩch — malds.
Tur tumꞩchi waꞧa ſtopi,
Wezs dſirnu akmens — galds.

Tur krodſineeks ꞩarkanu bahrdu,
Kà ꞩaulê apꞩarzis meſchs.
Wiꞥꞩch nes man wiꞩu beſ wahrdu:
Tam nawa weeꞩis ꞩweꞩchs.

No ganeem meitiꞥa pahrnahk,
Ꞩuns nogulſtas man pee kahjam.
Ta kluꞩi pee lodſiꞥa ꞩehſtas —
Man netihk wairs uſ mahjam.
