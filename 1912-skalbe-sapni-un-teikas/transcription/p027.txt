Rihta ſtunda

Kad baltâ rihta ſtundâ
Dahrſs ꞩaules miglâ dus,
Jauns roſchu kruhms pee loga
Steepj ſahrtus pumpurus.

Waj ꞩapnis nenodſiꞩis,
Waj n'eꞩi pamodees?
Kà baltâ ꞩaules miglâ
Wiꞩs tewi noꞩwehrees.

Ar ꞩaules miglu azis,
Ar ſeedu putekłeem, —
Tik gaiꞩchi leepu ſari
Nahk kà iſ mahkoꞥeem.

Ne lapa dreb, ne puꞣe,
Kas ſeedu wehruꞩi,
Dreb tawa ꞩirds weenweena,
Kà ꞩauli dſehruꞩi.
