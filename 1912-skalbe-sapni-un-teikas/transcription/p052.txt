Ledus puꞣe

    Uſ galda bahli ꞩweze ſtaro.
Plaukſt logâ ledus puꞣes ſeeds.
Tu nahz pee manis noꞩaluꞩi,
Man aukſtu bahlu roku ꞩneedſ.

    Kà gahle ſalgo ꞩeenas ſilas
Un kluꞩi ꞩaltas ehnas ꞩweeſch,
Kà diwas ꞩmagas ledus ꞩchꞣilas
Beſ walodas muhs mehmi ꞩpeeſch.

    Tu nahzi tà, kà ſilâ ruhtî
Nahk ledus ſeediꞥꞩch, wehja neſts.
Beſ krahꞩas tawa maigà ꞩeja,
Kà ꞩapnis bahlâ naktî dweſts.

    Es tawu aukſto roku ꞥehmu
Un ꞩildiju, pats ꞩadegdams,
Un gaidiju, kà brihnums notiks
Un leeꞩmâs mirdſès ꞩirds un nams.

    Waj mana ꞩirds pee tawas ꞩaꞩals?
Mirdſ ledus puꞣe aꞩarâs.
Waj manâs rokâs iſkuꞩuꞩi,
Kà ledus puꞣe aꞩarâs.
