Buht nabagam

    Buht nabagam, zik ꞩaldi ꞩchinî laikâ,
Kad katris wehrdiꞥu ar lihkeem pirkſteem dſen,
Kad ꞩaule nobahl ikdeenibas twaikâ,
Kà ꞩarmâ koks kad zilweks ſten.

    Buht nabagam, zik ꞩaldi ꞩchinî laikâ,
Kad wehrgi wehrgus wada deenas juhgâ,
Kur likteꞥi zits zitâ ꞩwehruꞩchees,
Kà bałꞣi ploſta ꞩmagâ ſtruhgâ.

    Buht nabagam, kad wiꞩs ir iſdſiꞩis,
Uſ altareem kad leeꞩmas nekwehpina.
Un ꞩwezi tikai tad wairs aiſdedſina,
Kad jauſmeklè graꞩis paſudis.

    Ar ꞩpeeeꞣi apmeſtees kaut kuꞧâ zeła malâ:
Te leekuliba, aukſtums ꞩirdi neꞩaꞩpeedìs.
Nakts tahłu augſtu ſilos wahrtus wehrs,
Pahr tewi ſwaigſchꞥu ꞩegu ꞩweedìs.
