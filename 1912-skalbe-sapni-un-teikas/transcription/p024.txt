Zeriꞥa kruhms

Wezs zeriꞥa kruhms uſ kapa,
Ik pawaꞩari ſeed;
Tu nowahrdſis garam ſtaigà
Un ſeedos ꞩeju ꞩpeed.

Wezs zeriꞥa kruhms uſ kapa,
Uſ kapa tu gribetu buht:
Tam ꞩaknes nahwes tumꞩâ,
Bet ſari ꞩauli juht —

Ar jaunu mihlu ꞩmarſchot
Wezos ꞩahpju pelnos
Un ſilus ſeedus ꞩchuhpot
Saros ꞩkumjos, melnos.
