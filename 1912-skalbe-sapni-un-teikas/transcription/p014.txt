II

    Kad es leezu gurdo peeri
Pahri ꞩchuhpuliꞩcha malam,
Weras ꞩirds man atkal dſeeꞩmam,
Ꞩaulei, pawaꞩara tꞩchalam:

    Man ſem noguruꞩchàm azim
Tek un aiſpluhſt ſili ſtrauti
Apeniꞩchi, wehja ꞩkauti,
Baltâs krihta klintîs lokas ...
Teku lihdſi maſàm kahjam
Tur uſ wezà ruhꞣa mahjam,
Kur pehz ꞩenàs paraꞩchas
Ꞩneedſ mums ꞩiltas karaꞩchas.
Teku lihdſi maſàm kahjam
Tur, gar wezà ruhꞣa mahjam,
Bitei puꞣês breenu lihdſ,
Ꞩkudrai ꞩaku: „Deews palihdſ!“
Teku lihdſi maſàm kahjam
Tur, gar wezà ruhꞣa mahjam,
Kur ſem lapam ꞩeenahſits
Deenwidâ beſ elpas ꞩihz.
Jauns ir lauks un jauns ir meſchs,
Un tik tuws, kas bija ꞩweꞩchs.
