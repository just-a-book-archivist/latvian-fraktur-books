Ꞩaules reets

Zik weegli tawa roka
Ꞩchàs wahrpas aiſꞩchuhpo.
Mehs ejam gar rudſu druwu,
Gar maigi ſilgano ..

Kad wehja dſirnawam kalnâ
Ꞩeen ꞩpahrnus noreets ꞩahrts,
Uſ tawàm luhpam un manàm
Top kluꞩs kahds neteikts wahrds.

Kà waꞧawihkꞩnas ꞩpulgo
Tur ꞩpahrnu ſehꞡelês,
Met zeema logi leeꞩmas
Ꞩew ꞩalmu paꞩpahrnês.

Pee tawas baltàs rokas
Ꞩuns peenahk, mekledams glahſta,
Es leezos pehz zełmalas ſeeda
Itkà ſem ꞩalda lahſta.

Es ꞩawu galwu liktu,
Kad wahrpas noraꞩo,
Lai tawa lehnà roka
Kà wahrpu to aiſꞩchuhpo.
