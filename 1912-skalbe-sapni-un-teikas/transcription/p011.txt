Manam puikam

    Prom pa meſcheem, prom pa laukeem
Maſais dehls mans eet kà wilziꞥꞩch.
Ꞩeptiꞥjuhdſchu ſahbaziꞥi
Wiꞥam nobrauꞥatâs kahjâs.
Prom pa kalneem, prom pa lejam,
Waj aiſ plezeem ꞩpahrni ꞩpurdſa?
Meſcha putni, balodiꞩchi
Ꞩpurdſ ap wiꞥa balto galwu.
Mahkoniꞩchi zełu rahda.
Pura puꞣes, pureniꞩchi
Miglâ ſwana ſelta ſwanus,
Swana, wił uſ ſtrauta malu.
Un, ſem kahrkleem behgułojot,
Silas wiłꞥu lihdaziꞥas
Ꞩmejas: Puika, laidees lihdſi
Mums uſ ſilo Daugawiꞥu.
Prom pa pławam, prom pa grawam —
Mati ꞩaules wehjâ wilnì —
Tur, uſ ſilo Daugawiꞥu. ...
Leepu laiwa, leepu laiwa,
Tezi weegli manam ſehnam
Pahri bruhneem Daugawwiłꞥeem,
Tur, uſ ſilu juhꞧas pili.
