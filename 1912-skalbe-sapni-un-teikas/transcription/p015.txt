Ꞡiꞡis brauz uſ meega muiſchu

Miglas muiſcha — meega muiſcha ...
Breen pa ꞩmilti kumeliꞥꞩch.
Egles aijà, behrſi aijà,
Aijà, zeła brauzejiꞥꞩch!

Wirꞩchi ꞩmarꞩcho raꞩas pilni,
Eſers rahmi ꞩchuhpo wilni.
Mirgà ſelta uguntiꞥꞩch,
Lehni lingà pulkſtentiꞥꞩch.

Miglas muiſcha, meega muiſcha,
Nedim ratiꞥi uſ tilta,
Migla nahk, kà elpa ꞩilta.
Uhdens puꞣes baltas, lehnas
Noleez ratos galwas, ehnas
Gruhſtas, ꞩaka: Pawedeet,
Mums ar zełꞩch uſ muiſchu eet.
Aptur Ꞡiꞡim ratiꞥus.
Puiꞩis aiſwer plakſtiꞥus ...
