Pirtiꞥa

Pee purwiꞥa, kur dihꞣis ꞩmagi ſeed,
No ſemes pirtiꞥa kà ꞩehne ahrâ leed.
Kà welena uſ jumta ſałi bruhna
Aug piku pikeem mihkſta ꞩamta ꞩuhna.
Ar pakꞩcheem ꞩaꞩchkeebteem, pusſakrituſi
Ꞩnauſch buhda, lupſtajos lihdſ jumtam eegrimuꞩi.
Pee durwim ꞩehrmuhkꞩlis ar jauneem ogu tꞩchemeem
Tur ſargà pirtiꞥu no purwa gareem ſemeem.
Tur azinai ꞩen iſbrukuꞩchi grodi,
Tur pelnos apgahſts pods, lai neapꞩlehpjas jodi ...

Eet deenwidâ tur garam mana taka,
Kà burwja azs ꞩpihd glumos kokos aka.
Es ꞩchꞣiru lupſtajus un ꞩmarꞩcha apꞥem mani,
Ꞩahk ꞩranet ꞩenatne, kà tahłi ſelta ſwani ..
Es peeri peeꞩpeeſchu pee tumꞩchà loga ſtikla,
Kà ꞩapnî azs top manim mikla:
No ſelta biꞣera tur pati Laima dſer
Un tumꞩà liktenus ar baltu roku ꞩwer.
