Ꞩila pulkſteniꞩchi

Bahli ꞩila pulkſteniꞩchi,
Kas ſem eglem kluꞩi ꞩapꞥo, —
Kam tik bahlas wiꞥu krahꞩas?
Reti meſcha dſiłâ krehꞩlâ
Paweras teem debeꞩs bahla.
Ilgodamees uſlaꞩija
Pulkſteniꞩchi debeꞩs gaiꞩmu.

Bahli ꞩila pulkſteniꞩchi,
Kas ſem eglem kluꞩi ꞩapꞥo...
Kur gan der ꞩchee pulkſteniꞩchi?
Leeli meſchi der preekꞩch malkas,
Kam ꞩchee meſcha pulkſteniꞩchi?

Pat ir ſaꞣis wiꞥu neehd —
Tam ir ꞩawi kahpoſtiꞥi.
Lahzis weenaldſigi ꞩamin,
Gleemeſs kuhtri garam welkas
Tur uſ treknàm, glumàm ꞩehnem.

Bahli ꞩila pulkſteniꞩchi,
Kas ſem eglem kluꞩi ꞩapꞥo,
Uſꞣer tahłu ſwaigſchꞥu ſtarus,
Wer kà mehles ꞩawos ſwanos.
Wehꞩma kad ſem eglem ſtaigà,
Wiꞥi ꞩahk tik kluꞩi ꞩkanet.
Kur gan der tik kluꞩi ſwani?
Weentuligai meſcha malai,
Ganam, deenwidâ kas ꞩapꞥo,
Galwu noleezis uſ ꞩuhnam.
