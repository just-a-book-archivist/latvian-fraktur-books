# Kārlis Skalbe: *Kā es braucu Ziemeļmeitas lūkoties* (1911)

*Latviski:* Kārļa Skalbes pasaka „Kā es braucu Ziemeļmeitas lūkoties“, diplomātiska transkripcija
vecajā rakstībā. Pirmā un otrā korektūra ir pabeigtas:
visi 6873 vārdi un visas 1082 rindas ir salīdzinātas ar skenējumu.

A literary fairy tale in prose by Kārlis Skalbe (1879–1945), "How I went to woo the Northern
Maiden".

The print spells the title `brauzu`; the folder name follows the modern *braucu*.

## Layout of the files

The general rules are in [`../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md`](../EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md). In this
book:

- **Paragraphs** begin with four spaces, after a blank line.
- **Verse** within the prose: one line of verse per line, eight spaces indent, a blank line before
  and after.
- **Section break:** the three asterisks of the print (two above, one below in the middle) are
  written as two lines, `*   *` and `  *`, with a blank line before and after.

## The scans

`pages.tsv` gives the image for every printed page: page *p* is image *p* + 8 of the Internet
Archive's JPEG 2000 set (`kaesbraucuzeemel00skaluoft_jp2.zip`), e.g. p. 4 =
`kaesbraucuzeemel00skaluoft_0012.jp2`.
